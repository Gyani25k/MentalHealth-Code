Year at a Glance

Each month carries a dedicated theme with targeted activities, awareness days & creative interventions

01
❄️ Theme: New Beginnings & Cold-Weather Resilience
January
📚 Jan 24 – International Day of Education
🎯
Goal-Setting Without Burnout Workshop
Interactive session on setting SMART goals with built-in rest periods. Includes worksheet and peer check-ins to prevent overcommitment at year-start.
Workshop
🌩️
Debunking Myths About Seasonal Depression
Awareness session with infographics dispelling common myths about SAD (Seasonal Affective Disorder). Cold-weather mood changes normalised through expert talk.
Awareness
🎓
Education & Intersectional Wellbeing — Jan 24
On International Day of Education, a panel discussion linking mental health with academic equity across gender, caste, disability, and socioeconomic backgrounds.
Panel · Jan 24
🏆
Interschool Mental Health Competition
Poster-making and resolution-writing competition open across school departments. Theme: "A Mind That Matters." Winners featured on campus board.
Competition
📜
Wellbeing Resolution Writing
Students write and share one personal mental health resolution for the new year. Displayed anonymously on a "Resolution Wall" in the student commons.
Creative
🧘
Morning Meditation Series
Guided 15-minute morning meditation every Friday in January. Specially designed for cold mornings with breathing exercises and body-scan techniques.
Wellbeing
02
💕 Theme: Relationships, Connection & Belonging
February
🌍 Feb 5 – Group Diversity Day (Wed) 💙 Kindness Week
🌍
Group Diversity Session — Feb 5 (Wednesday)
Structured group activity celebrating cultural, social and identity diversity. Students share one thing unique about their background in a safe facilitated space.
Inclusion · Feb 5
💛
Kindness Week Campaign
One week of structured kindness challenges — writing notes to classmates, helping a stranger, surprise compliments. Documented on a shared gratitude board.
Campaign
🎲
Random Acts of Kindness Day
Campus-wide event where students receive anonymous "kindness task" cards. Tasks range from sharing lunch to leaving a positive sticky note on a library book.
Event
🍽️
Eating Disorders Awareness Session
Led by a nutrition counsellor and psychologist. Covers signs of disordered eating, body image pressures, and how peers can support without triggering. Flashcard takeaway.
Health · Awareness
🃏
Mental Health Flashcard Toolkit
Design and distribute sets of illustrated flashcards with coping techniques, breathing exercises, and self-compassion affirmations. Student-designed entries shortlisted.
Creative Resource
🎙️
Podcast Talk Show — "Let's Talk"
Live recorded student-hosted talk show with two episodes in February. Episode 1: Relationships and mental health. Episode 2: When love hurts — toxic dynamics. Available campus-wide.
Media · Expression
03
🌿 Theme: Digital Wellbeing, Sleep Health & Body Awareness
March
🌐 Digital Detox Week ⚠️ Self-Harm Awareness Month
📵
Digital Detox Challenge
One-week challenge with daily offline tasks: journaling, outdoor walks, cooking. Students track screen time before and after and share their experience on a notice board.
Challenge
🎨
Digital Detox Face-Painting Event
Fun offline creative event where students get face-painted with nature, mindfulness, and calm-themed designs. Symbolic unplugging moment. Photography allowed post-event.
Creative Event
😴
Sleep Health Awareness Week
Displays, quizzes, and a counsellor-led session on sleep hygiene, circadian rhythm, and how poor sleep worsens anxiety and academic performance.
Awareness
🤍
Self-Harm Awareness Day
Safe, sensitively facilitated session. White ribbon awareness, peer support circles, and resource sharing. Focus on safe messaging, not triggering discussion of methods.
Sensitive · Awareness
💊
Drug Awareness Programme
Collaboration with health department: interactive session on substance use, peer pressure, and help-seeking. Includes a myth-busting quiz and helpline resource card distribution.
Awareness · Health
📊
Digital Wellness Poster Exhibition
Student-created posters on healthy tech habits, blue light, social media comparison, and FOMO. Displayed in corridors with QR codes linking to wellbeing resources.
Exhibition
04
🌻 Theme: Stress Awareness & Coping Strategies
April
📅 Stress Awareness Month
🧠
Stress Awareness Month Launch
Opening assembly with statistics on student stress, causes, and the mind-body connection. A "Stress Scale" self-assessment distributed to all first-year students.
Launch Event
🖼️
Stress Management Poster Competition
Campus-wide poster-making on themes: academic pressure, time management, and healthy coping. Winning posters printed and displayed across departments.
Competition
🎭
Nukkad Natak — Street Play
Student theatre groups perform 10–15 minute street plays on stress, academic burnout, and seeking help. Performed in canteen area and main plaza. Open to all.
Theatre · Outreach
🤫
Hour of Silence — Mindful Pause
A designated hour where a section of campus becomes a quiet zone. Students encouraged to sit, reflect, read, or breathe without phones. Facilitated by counsellors.
Mindfulness
📋
Stress Toolkit Distribution
Printed stress-management guides with techniques: progressive muscle relaxation, journaling prompts, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, and study break schedules.
Resource
🤝
Peer Support Circle
Small facilitated groups where students share stress experiences under counsellor guidance. Emphasis on listening without judgment and affirming coping strategies.
Peer Support
05
💚 Theme: Mental Health Matters — Expression & Listening
May
🌍 May 10 – World Mental Health Day (Pre-event)
🔖
Mental Health Bookmarks
Student-designed bookmarks with mental health affirmations, helpline numbers, and breathing exercises. Distributed in the library and during exams.
Creative Resource
💌
Small Cards with Mental Health Messages
"You matter" card campaign — students design and anonymously place small cards with uplifting messages in classrooms, hostels, and washrooms across campus.
Campaign
👂
Listening Circles by Professionals
Organised structured listening sessions led by clinical psychologists and counsellors. Small groups (8–10 students). Confidential, judgment-free space. Pre-registration required.
Professional Session
🎨
Art Therapy Open Studio
Drop-in creative space with paints, collage, and clay. No art skills needed. Focused on processing emotions through unstructured creative expression.
Expression
📝
Letter to My Future Self
Students write letters about their mental health hopes and fears. Sealed, timestamped, returned at year-end. Promotes self-reflection and longitudinal wellbeing awareness.
Reflection
🌱
Mental Health Garden Walk
Guided mindful walk through campus green spaces. Each plant station paired with a mental health concept: roots = stability, leaves = growth, flowers = joy.
Wellbeing
06
☀️ Theme: Resilience, Identity & Summer Transitions
June
🌈 Jun 28 – Pride Month close
🪞
Identity & Self-Worth Workshop
Facilitated workshop on how identity, social roles, and external validation affect mental health. Includes individual reflection cards and group sharing.
Workshop
💪
Resilience Building Session
Evidence-based session on bounce-back strategies, post-failure recovery, and growth mindset. Includes a "My Resilience Map" personal exercise.
Skills
🌅
Transition Anxiety Support
Targeted for final-year students: navigating post-college transitions, fear of the unknown, and managing change anxiety. Peer alumni speakers invited.
Support Session
🎤
Student Voice Open Mic
Open mic on mental health stories, poetry, spoken word. Safe, non-judgemental platform. Trigger warnings announced before sensitive pieces.
Expression
07
🟣 Theme: Equality, Safety & Gender Sensitisation
July
⚖️ Equality & Safety Focus
⚥
Gender Sensitisation Workshop — I
Interactive session on gender stereotypes, unconscious bias, and how rigid gender norms harm mental health across all identities. Facilitated by a trained counsellor with case studies.
Workshop
🛡️
Campus Safety Awareness Session
Covers physical and psychological safety on campus — POSH Act, harassment reporting, and bystander intervention. Representatives from ICC present.
Awareness · Policy
🎭
Gender Sensitisation Role-Play Activity
Students enact scenarios involving gender-based assumptions and workplace/campus dynamics. Debrief led by counsellor to unpack emotions and biases surfaced.
Activity
📖
Equality Reading Corner
Curated book and article display in the library on gender, identity, and mental health. Monthly "recommendation card" by student volunteers for peers to discover new perspectives.
Resource
🌐
Online Pledge — Safe Campus Commitment
Online pledge drive where students commit to being an ally, challenging discrimination, and maintaining a safe environment. Certificate of participation generated digitally.
Digital Campaign
08
🌹 Theme: Grief Awareness & Accumulated Loss
August
🕊️ Grief Awareness Focus 🌿 Online Pledge Drive
🌳
Remembrance Tree Installation
A symbolic remembrance tree in the student common area where students hang paper leaves with names or notes honouring those they have lost. Supportive atmosphere maintained by counsellors.
Memorial · Healing
💔
Grief & Accumulated Loss Workshop
Workshop on how cumulative stress and unprocessed grief manifest in academic and social life. Includes psychoeducation on types of grief, disenfranchised grief, and support pathways.
Workshop
🕯️
Candlelight Remembrance Evening
Quiet, structured evening event honouring personal and collective losses. Soft music, candles, and an optional sharing circle facilitated by a counsellor in a supportive atmosphere.
Memorial Event
✍️
Letters of Release
Students write unsent letters to people they have lost or situations they are grieving. Letters are privately burned or folded into the remembrance tree as a symbolic act of letting go.
Expression · Healing
📱
Online Pledge — Nasha Mukt Bharat Support
Advance registration for November's Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan. Students pledge support via online form and receive early awareness material on substance use and mental health.
Digital Pledge
09
💙 Theme: Suicide Prevention — Look Out for Each Other
September
🎗️ Sep 10 – World Suicide Prevention Day
🚩
Flag the Signs — Warning Sign Awareness
Training session for students and faculty on recognising warning signs of distress and suicidal ideation. Uses the QPR (Question, Persuade, Refer) framework. Certificate issued.
Training · Sep 10
🚶
Walk of Hope
Campus walkathon on World Suicide Prevention Day. Students walk silently for 10 minutes in solidarity, carrying placards with mental health affirmations. Media coverage invited.
March · Sep 10
🛣️
Road March for Mental Health
Community awareness march through university campus and adjacent streets. Participants wear yellow ribbons. Local NGO mental health partner invited to lead.
Outreach
🩺
Mental Health First Aid Training
Full-day certified Mental Health First Aid (MHFA India) training for 25 student volunteers. Covers crisis response, active listening, and referral to professional help.
Certification · Training
💬
Brave Talk Campaign
"Brave Talk" is a structured conversation initiative where students are paired to have an honest check-in conversation guided by five questions about their current mental state. Debrief with counsellor available.
Campaign
🎗️
Yellow Ribbon Pledge Drive
Students receive yellow ribbons and pledge to be the person someone can talk to. Pledges written on ribbon stubs and pinned on a collective board: "You can talk to me."
Awareness · Pledge
10
🟠 Theme: Bullying Prevention & Neurodiversity Awareness
October
🎗️ Oct 10 – World Mental Health Day 💛 ADHD Awareness Month 💙 Anti-Bullying Week
🛑
Bullying Prevention Campaign
Week-long awareness drive with definition-busting, reporting mechanism briefings, and a student-created anti-bullying pledge wall. Covers cyberbullying, academic bullying, and social exclusion.
Campaign
📸
Instagram Mental Health Page Launch
Launch and ongoing management of an official student-run Instagram page for the Mental Health Cell. Weekly content: tips, awareness days, activity highlights, and anonymous student stories.
Social Media
🧩
ADHD Awareness Exhibition
Display boards and interactive games to demystify ADHD — how it presents in college students, how it is misread as laziness, and accommodations that work. ADHD-diagnosed speakers invited.
Awareness
📋
Anonymous Slip Box
Ongoing collection of anonymous slips where students report experiences of bullying or ask for mental health support. Reviewed weekly by the counsellor. Responses posted publicly (anonymised).
Listening Tool
🪑
ADHD Student Chaupal
"Chaupal" — an informal open sitting where students with ADHD or suspected ADHD share their academic and social challenges. No formal agenda. Attended by a counsellor as a resource person.
Community Circle
🌐
World Mental Health Day — Oct 10
Central campus event: stalls, awareness games, counsellor consultations, resource distribution, and a panel on the year's WHO theme. Press coverage invited.
Flagship · Oct 10
11
🟢 Theme: Men's Mental Health, Gratitude & Nasha Mukt Bharat
November
💙 Movember — Men's Mental Health 🇮🇳 Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan
👨
Men's Mental Health Awareness Campaign
Addressing the stigma that prevents men from seeking help. Includes posters, a panel discussion with male counsellors and student speakers, and a "It's okay to not be okay" wall.
Movember Campaign
🧡
Gratitude Wall
A large gratitude wall installed in the main corridor. Students write what they are grateful for on coloured paper strips and pin them on. Wall grows across the month.
Expression
🤧
Handkerchief Gratitude Campaign
Every student receives a handkerchief. They write one message of gratitude or care on it, then exchange it with someone they appreciate. A tangible, personal act of connection.
Signature Activity
🚫💊
Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan
University-level campaign event under the national Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan. Pledge-taking ceremony, awareness session on substance use and mental health, and collaboration with district health authority.
National Campaign
📖
Gratitude Journaling Challenge
21-day gratitude journaling challenge launched in November. Students record three things daily. End-of-month reflection session with counsellor support.
Wellbeing Practice
💙
Man Up? No — Let It Out.
Workshop specifically for male-identifying students. Challenges toxic masculinity norms around emotional expression and help-seeking. Led by a male psychologist.
Workshop
12
✨ Theme: Individuality, Individual Needs & Year Reflection
December
🎗️ Dec 1 – World AIDS Day 🌟 End-of-Year Reflections
🎗️
AIDS Awareness Day — Dec 1
Red ribbon distribution, awareness quiz, and a destigmatisation session on HIV/AIDS and its intersection with mental health — depression, stigma, isolation. Health counsellor invited.
Awareness · Dec 1
🪞
Individuality Celebration Showcase
Students create a one-page "Who I Am" visual profile — their strengths, quirks, support needs, and mental health journey in 2025. Displayed with consent. Celebrates neurodiversity and uniqueness.
Expression
📬
Letters to Self — Return & Reflect
Letters written in May are returned. Students read their past hopes and compare with their actual experience. A facilitated reflection session on growth, setbacks, and resilience closes the year.
Reflection
📣
Student Recommendations Forum
Open forum where students submit recommendations for next year's calendar — anonymously or named. All suggestions are read, compiled, and acknowledged by the Mental Health Cell team.
Participatory Planning
📊
Year-End Wellbeing Survey
Campus-wide survey assessing changes in mental health awareness, help-seeking behaviour, and peer support quality from August to December. Results shared transparently with students.
Evaluation
🎉
Wellness Champion Awards
Annual recognition for students, staff, and departments who contributed most to the mental health culture of the university. Nominations open to all. Celebrated in a small ceremony.
Celebration
